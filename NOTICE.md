## Images and models

PCB images are EDA exports/renders, not photographs of manufactured hardware.

The historical aircraft mesh combines frame and component CAD with concept models. Complete redistribution coverage has not been established, so `preview/assets/assembly.bin`, `preview/assets/assembly.json`, the original assembly BREP and third-party frame CAD remain excluded.
